Every sample here is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): free to use,
change and sell, commercially or not, with no attribution required. We
credit the authors anyway. Each was trimmed to its first shot or blast,
high-passed at 35 Hz, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, faded out, and encoded
as 48 kHz mono MP3.
